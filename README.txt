NITHYA SHREE S — PORTFOLIO

Design direction: "Model Card" — a dark ink/amber theme styled around
the AI/ML world's own "model card" idea, with a serif display face
(Fraunces), clean body text (Inter), and monospace data labels
(JetBrains Mono) for stats, dates and tags. A thin dotted "pipeline"
line runs down the left margin on desktop, and the closing Contact
section inverts to a warm paper tone as a final visual beat.

PHOTO:
Place your photo at:
images/photo.jpg
(same folder as index.html)

Folder structure:
    index.html
    style.css
    script.js
    images/
        photo.jpg

Open index.html in a browser. No build step required.

Fonts (Fraunces, Inter, JetBrains Mono) load from Google Fonts via
the @import at the top of style.css — an internet connection is
needed the first time a browser renders the page.
